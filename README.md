# 🦶 Bigfoot Sightings Power BI Dashboard

Welcome to the **Bigfoot Sightings Dashboard**, a visually compelling Power BI report designed to analyze and explore reported Bigfoot sightings across various geographies and time periods. This project combines publicly available data and intuitive design to help uncover patterns, anomalies, and trends in one of North America's most enduring legends.

---

## 🔍 Project Overview

This Power BI report provides a comprehensive and interactive dashboard that visualizes Bigfoot sightings based on:
- 📍 **Location** (State, Region, Latitude & Longitude)
- 🗓️ **Date of Sighting**
- ⏱️ **Time of Day**
- 🧍 **Witness Reports & Descriptions**
- ☁️ **Weather/Environmental Conditions** (if available)

The goal is to enable both enthusiasts and analysts to dive into the data and extract meaningful insights into **when**, **where**, and **how** sightings tend to occur.

---

## 📊 Key Features

- **Geo-mapping of Sightings**  
  Interactive maps showing clustering of sightings by region, with drill-down capabilities to explore individual reports.

- **Time-Series Analysis**  
  Trends of sightings across months and years. Are there peak seasons or decades for Bigfoot?

- **Text Analysis of Reports**  
  Basic analysis of keywords and common phrases in witness descriptions (e.g., size, color, behavior).

- **Slicer Filters**  
  Dynamic filters for state, year, time of day, and sighting classification, enabling custom exploration.

- **Custom Visuals**  
  Incorporates rich custom visuals for storytelling and intuitive navigation between insights.

---

## 🧠 Insights & Discoveries

> Example insights revealed in this dashboard:
- Sightings appear to **peak in summer months**, possibly due to more outdoor activity.
- The **Pacific Northwest** dominates in report volume, but there are surprising outliers in the Midwest.
- Several clusters of high-credibility reports originate near national parks and wilderness reserves.

## 🚀 Getting Started

To run the report:

1. Open `Bigfoot Sightings - Final.pbix` in Power BI Desktop.
